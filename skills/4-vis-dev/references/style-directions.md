# Style Directions — Reference Library

Two-layer system. **Medium** is what the image is made of. **Aesthetic** is how it feels. Pick one from each — the combination IS the style.

`Medium × Aesthetic = Style Lock`

Neo-noir × anime. Villeneuve × 3D CG. Wes Anderson × stop motion. Any combo is valid.

Used by `4-style-lock.md` to present style options and generate comparison prompts.

---

# LAYER 1: MEDIUM

What is this made of? The physical/technical foundation.

---

## LIVE ACTION

### LA-1. CINEMATIC LIVE ACTION
**What it is:** Real cameras, real light, real actors. Film or high-end digital.
**Prompt DNA:** `cinematic photography, shallow depth of field, anamorphic lens, film grain, 35mm, natural lighting`
**Best for:** Grounded worlds, adult audiences, prestige, awards track.
**Limits:** VFX-heavy worlds get expensive fast, creature design hits uncanny valley.

### LA-2. ANALOG FILM
**What it is:** Specific film stocks as visual identity. The grain, the color science, the imperfection IS the medium.
**Prompt DNA:** `vintage film stock, heavy grain, muted warm palette, light leaks, soft focus edges, Kodak Portra, 16mm`
**Best for:** Period, nostalgia, memory-frame, indie.
**Limits:** Retro costume risk, limited to certain eras/tones.

### LA-3. DOCUMENTARY / FOUND FOOTAGE
**What it is:** The camera shouldn't be here. Phone quality, surveillance, vérité.
**Prompt DNA:** `security camera footage, phone camera quality, low resolution, timestamp overlay, surveillance aesthetic, night vision`
**Best for:** Horror, immediacy, low-budget high-concept.
**Limits:** Narrow range, audience fatigue, beauty-resistant.

---

## 2D ANIMATION

### 2D-1. CLASSIC HAND-DRAWN
**What it is:** Ink and paint. The human hand in every line. Cel animation.
**Prompt DNA:** `traditional 2D animation, hand-drawn, cel-shaded, painterly backgrounds, ink and paint, expressive linework`
**Best for:** Emotional range, all-ages, franchise, timeless feel.
**Limits:** Labor-intensive, "kids' stuff" perception.

### 2D-2. ANIME
**What it is:** Japanese animation grammar. Specific conventions for eyes, movement, framing, speed.
**Prompt DNA:** `anime style, bold outlines, dramatic lighting, dynamic composition, Japanese animation`
**Sub-variants:** Shonen (kinetic, bold), Seinen (detailed, mature), Mecha, Slice-of-life. Specify in aesthetic layer.
**Best for:** Action, massive global fandom, multi-season endurance, merch.
**Limits:** Formulaic risk if not directed, cultural specificity can be a barrier.

### 2D-3. GRAPHIC NOVEL / COMIC BOOK
**What it is:** Ink-dominant. Panels, gutters, halftone. The page as visual grammar.
**Prompt DNA:** `comic book illustration, heavy ink lines, halftone dots, bold shadows, graphic novel style, Ben-Day dots`
**Best for:** IP cross-platform, print+screen native, strong visual identity.
**Limits:** Can flatten emotion, some audiences resist.

### 2D-4. LIGNE CLAIRE
**What it is:** Clean uniform outlines, flat color, no hatching. European comic precision.
**Prompt DNA:** `ligne claire style, clean uniform outlines, flat color fills, no hatching, minimal shadow, European comic art`
**Best for:** Sophisticated, print-perfect, ages well.
**Limits:** Cold, limited atmosphere, niche.

### 2D-5. FLAT / GEOMETRIC
**What it is:** Shapes first. Every form reduced to essential geometry. Graphic design as animation.
**Prompt DNA:** `flat geometric illustration, bold shapes, limited palette, graphic design aesthetic, strong silhouettes, minimalist`
**Best for:** Iconic, scalable, merch, all-ages, animation-efficient.
**Limits:** Limited emotional subtlety, hard to do gritty.

### 2D-6. ROTOSCOPE
**What it is:** Traced live action. Real movement, unreal surface. The uncanny shimmer.
**Prompt DNA:** `rotoscope animation, traced live action, painterly overlay, shifting outlines, dreamlike movement`
**Best for:** Surreal, altered states, adult audience, dream logic.
**Limits:** Production-heavy, audience resistance, limited commercial track.

### 2D-7. WATERCOLOR / PAINTERLY
**What it is:** Edges bleed. Color lives. Every frame is a painting in progress.
**Prompt DNA:** `watercolor illustration, soft bleeding edges, painterly texture, visible brushstrokes, delicate color washes`
**Best for:** Beauty, warmth, art-world crossover, pastoral.
**Limits:** Hard in action, production cost, niche.

### 2D-8. WOODBLOCK / LINOCUT
**What it is:** Carved marks. Limited color. The resistance of the material IS the style.
**Prompt DNA:** `woodblock print style, bold carved lines, limited color palette, textured ink impression, high contrast, relief print`
**Best for:** Striking, tactile, historical resonance.
**Limits:** Very niche, limited expression, animation-resistant.

## 3D / CG ANIMATION

### 3D-1. PIXAR / DISNEY CG
**What it is:** Polished, physically based rendering. Subsurface scattering, volumetric light. Emotion at industrial scale.
**Prompt DNA:** `3D animated, Pixar style, polished CG, subsurface scattering, expressive character design, volumetric lighting, physically based materials`
**Best for:** Mass audience, franchise engine, all-ages, global.
**Limits:** Expensive, formula fatigue, "Pixar face," hard to be truly strange.

### 3D-2. SPIDER-VERSE
**What it is:** CG with hand-drawn overlay, halftone, variable frame rate. Print artifacts as features. Every frame a poster.
**Prompt DNA:** `Spider-Verse style, CG with hand-drawn overlay, halftone dots, comic book textures, paint splatter, bold graphic lines over 3D`
**Best for:** Revolutionary identity, youth audience, critical respect.
**Limits:** Everyone copying it, production complexity, tone-specific.

### 3D-3. ARCANE / PAINTERLY CG
**What it is:** CG with a painter's hand. Hand-finished textures. Oil painting meets render farm.
**Prompt DNA:** `painterly 3D animation, hand-painted textures, visible brushstrokes on CG, oil painting lighting, stylized CG with traditional finish`
**Best for:** Premium, adult-crossover, game/IP adaptation, distinctive.
**Limits:** Studio-dependent, production cost, hard to replicate.

### 3D-4. HYPERREAL CG
**What it is:** Photorealistic rendering. You can't tell it's not real. Digital cinematography.
**Prompt DNA:** `photorealistic CGI, hyperreal rendering, ray-traced lighting, physically accurate materials, volumetric atmosphere`
**Best for:** Spectacle, immersion, live-action hybrid.
**Limits:** Uncanny valley, soulless without authorship, very expensive.

### 3D-5. STOP MOTION
**What it is:** Fingerprints visible. Every frame a sculpture. Miniature sets, practical light. Imperfection is the magic.
**Prompt DNA:** `stop motion animation, miniature sets, handcrafted textures, visible puppet joints, practical lighting, tactile materials`
**Best for:** Tactile charm, authorship, awards, distinctive.
**Limits:** Extremely slow, scale limitations, limited action.

---

## HYBRID / MIXED MEDIA

### HY-1. LIVE ACTION + ANIMATION
**What it is:** Two realities in one frame. The drawn and the photographed coexist.
**Prompt DNA:** `mixed media, live action with animation overlay, hand-drawn characters in real environment, hybrid`
**Best for:** Novel, comic, IP bridging.
**Limits:** Tone management, gimmick risk.

### HY-2. COLLAGE / MIXED MEDIA
**What it is:** Cut and paste. Nothing born in the same place. The seams are the art.
**Prompt DNA:** `mixed media collage, cut paper textures, layered photographs, analog collage, visible seams, scrapbook aesthetic`
**Best for:** Punk energy, distinctive, cheap-high-impact, social-native.
**Limits:** Feature-length endurance, "art project" risk.

### HY-3. AI-AUGMENTED / GENERATIVE
**What it is:** The machine's hand is visible. Glitch as grammar. New visual territory.
**Prompt DNA:** `AI generated aesthetic, dreamlike morphing, generative textures, surreal photorealism, fluid forms, machine artifacts as style`
**Best for:** Cutting edge, speed, conversation-starting.
**Limits:** Skepticism, ethical debate, coherence issues, ages fast.

# LAYER 2: AESTHETIC

How does it feel? The tonal/visual approach applied ON TOP of the medium. Organized into three categories: **Director Signatures**, **Movements**, and **Cultural Aesthetics**.

---

## DIRECTOR SIGNATURES

Visual grammars defined by a specific filmmaker's eye. Apply to any medium.

### DIR-1. VILLENEUVE
**Feel:** Monumental scale, precise composition, earned awe. The human body against impossible architecture. Silence as score.
**Prompt DNA:** `Denis Villeneuve style, monumental scale, vast landscapes, precise symmetrical composition, muted earth palette, golden hour, atmospheric haze, anamorphic widescreen, epic but intimate`
**References:** Dune, Blade Runner 2049, Arrival, Sicario.
**Signature moves:** Scale contrast (tiny human / massive structure), horizon-dominant composition, amber-to-steel color shifts, fog/dust/haze as character.

### DIR-2. FINCHER
**Feel:** Surgical. Every frame is evidence. The camera knows something the characters don't. Sickly precision.
**Prompt DNA:** `David Fincher style, cold precision, desaturated palette, institutional lighting, overhead shots, forensic detail, controlled camera, procedural aesthetic`
**References:** Zodiac, Se7en, Mindhunter, Gone Girl, Fight Club.
**Signature moves:** Flat institutional light, sickly green/yellow undertone, overhead God shots, tracking through architecture, the camera never flinches.

### DIR-3. WES ANDERSON
**Feel:** Symmetry as worldview. Pastel dollhouse. Every frame a diorama. Grief dressed in whimsy.
**Prompt DNA:** `Wes Anderson style, perfect symmetry, centered composition, pastel palette, flat staging, dollhouse miniature feel, whimsical production design, planimetric framing`
**References:** Grand Budapest Hotel, Moonrise Kingdom, Royal Tenenbaums, Isle of Dogs.
**Signature moves:** Dead-center framing, cutaway cross-sections, text-as-design, pan-and-scan lateral movement, color-coded characters.

### DIR-4. KUBRICK
**Feel:** The one-point perspective into madness. Geometric perfection hiding violence. The uncanny mechanical.
**Prompt DNA:** `Stanley Kubrick style, one-point perspective, symmetrical corridors, cold clinical lighting, unsettling stillness, geometric precision, wide-angle distortion`
**References:** 2001, The Shining, A Clockwork Orange, Full Metal Jacket, Eyes Wide Shut.
**Signature moves:** Vanishing-point hallways, the Kubrick stare (head tilted, eyes up), overwhelming symmetry, red as violence, architecture as psychology.

### DIR-5. MIYAZAKI
**Feel:** Wind moves. Water is alive. Silence has weight. Every background is a painting. Nature as spirit.
**Prompt DNA:** `Hayao Miyazaki style, lush natural environments, watercolor atmosphere, soft light, pastoral detail, flight sequences, cloud formations, food rendered with love`
**References:** Spirited Away, Princess Mononoke, Nausicaa, Howl's Moving Castle.
**Signature moves:** Running through grass, clouds as characters, detailed food, flying machines, the quiet pause before action, nature reclaiming the built world.

### DIR-6. PARK CHAN-WOOK
**Feel:** Violence as choreography. Beauty that cuts. Every frame composed like a wound dressed in silk.
**Prompt DNA:** `Park Chan-wook style, jewel-tone palette, ornate violence, baroque composition, saturated color, theatrical lighting, meticulous production design, beauty and cruelty fused`
**References:** Oldboy, The Handmaiden, Stoker, Decision to Leave.
**Signature moves:** Overhead violence, split-screen as psychology, green as corruption, the beautiful container hiding the ugly truth, tracking shots through partitions.

### DIR-7. SAFDIE BROTHERS
**Feel:** Handheld panic. Sweat visible. The camera is an uninvited guest at a disaster.
**Prompt DNA:** `Safdie Brothers style, handheld urgency, neon-lit streets, shallow focus, tight close-ups, available light, gritty urban texture, claustrophobic framing, frenetic energy`
**References:** Uncut Gems, Good Time, Heaven Knows What.
**Signature moves:** Faces filling the frame under stress, neon as anxiety, overlapping dialogue, the camera can't keep up, New York as sensory assault.

### DIR-8. GUILLERMO DEL TORO
**Feel:** Monsters as metaphor. Gothic warmth. The creature IS the poetry. Amber and sapphire.
**Prompt DNA:** `Guillermo del Toro style, gothic fairy tale, amber and deep blue palette, intricate creature design, ornate darkness, clockwork mechanisms, warm candlelight, monstrous beauty`
**References:** Pan's Labyrinth, Shape of Water, Crimson Peak, Pacific Rim.
**Signature moves:** Amber key light, insects as motif, mechanical/organic fusion in creatures, fairy tale framing of horror, underground/underwater as womb.

### DIR-9. TAIKA WAITITI
**Feel:** Deadpan in widescreen. The comedy is in the composition. Warmth smuggled into absurdity.
**Prompt DNA:** `Taika Waititi style, deadpan wide shots, warm natural palette, absurd juxtaposition in frame, comedic staging, New Zealand landscape, irreverent framing of epic subjects`
**References:** Hunt for the Wilderpeople, Jojo Rabbit, What We Do in the Shadows, Thor Ragnarok.
**Signature moves:** Two-shot with scale difference, landscape as punchline, characters too small in frame, DIY production design played straight, the awkward pause held long.

### DIR-10. BONG JOON-HO
**Feel:** Social architecture as visual design. Vertical class. The frame literally divides rich from poor.
**Prompt DNA:** `Bong Joon-ho style, architectural class metaphor, vertical composition, precise blocking, naturalistic but heightened, genre-fluid tone, rain as metaphor, spatial hierarchy`
**References:** Parasite, Memories of Murder, Snowpiercer, Okja.
**Signature moves:** Stairs as class mobility, above/below framing, window frames within the frame, the moment genre shifts within a single scene, rain revealing what's hidden.

### DIR-11. JORDAN PEELE
**Feel:** Suburban dread in bright daylight. Horror that's about something. The familiar made wrong.
**Prompt DNA:** `Jordan Peele style, sunlit horror, suburban uncanny, bright palette with wrong undertone, wide symmetrical shots of normal spaces, eerie stillness, social horror`
**References:** Get Out, Us, Nope.
**Signature moves:** Daylight as horror, the too-perfect environment, underground/underside reveals, eyes as portals, Americana twisted, the long stare.

### DIR-12. COEN BROTHERS
**Feel:** God's-eye view of human folly. The landscape is indifferent. Violence arrives without warning. Dry as bone.
**Prompt DNA:** `Coen Brothers style, stark landscapes, deadpan composition, harsh natural light, dust and empty space, dark humor framing, Americana decay, long static shots`
**References:** No Country for Old Men, Fargo, Blood Simple, Barton Fink, The Big Lebowski.
**Signature moves:** The empty road, POV of the approaching threat, fluorescent-lit interiors, bodies in wrong places, weather as moral commentary.

### DIR-13. SPIKE LEE
**Feel:** The double dolly. The camera and the subject move together, floating through a world that argues with them.
**Prompt DNA:** `Spike Lee style, double dolly shots, Dutch angles, bold primary colors, confrontational direct-to-camera, urban Brooklyn texture, high contrast, theatrical blocking`
**References:** Do the Right Thing, Malcolm X, 25th Hour, BlacKkKlansman.
**Signature moves:** Direct address to camera, the double dolly float, canted angles as moral imbalance, red as heat/anger, the block as universe.

### DIR-14. RIDLEY SCOTT
**Feel:** Smoke and light. Massive interiors. Industrial grandeur. The future is a factory. Beauty in the machine.
**Prompt DNA:** `Ridley Scott style, smoky atmospheric lighting, industrial cathedral interiors, backlit silhouettes, massive scale architecture, rain and steam, blue-grey palette`
**References:** Blade Runner, Alien, Gladiator, Black Hawk Down, The Duellists.
**Signature moves:** Backlit smoke/rain, industrial interiors as cathedrals, the slow reveal of scale, lens flares through atmosphere, the body as small thing in vast space.

### DIR-15. KUROSAWA
**Feel:** Weather IS drama. The human figure against the storm. Movement as composition. The wipe.
**Prompt DNA:** `Akira Kurosawa style, dramatic weather, bold human figures against sky, dynamic movement composition, high contrast black and white, epic tableau, wind and rain as character`
**References:** Seven Samurai, Rashomon, Ran, Yojimbo, Throne of Blood.
**Signature moves:** Rain before battle, wind bending trees and cloth, the wipe transition, figures silhouetted against sky, depth staging with foreground/background action.

### DIR-16. WONG KAR-WAI
**Feel:** Longing in neon. The blur of missed connection. Time stretched like taffy. Color as heartbreak.
**Prompt DNA:** `Wong Kar-wai style, smeared neon light, step-printed slow motion, saturated jewel tones, cramped interiors, reflections in glass, cigarette smoke, nostalgic blur`
**References:** In the Mood for Love, Chungking Express, Happy Together, 2046.
**Signature moves:** Slow-motion in tight spaces, reflections multiplying the subject, clocks and dates as motif, red and green opposition, the back of the head, corridors.

### DIR-17. DENIS HOPPER / 70s NEW HOLLYWOOD
**Feel:** America on film before anyone cleaned it up. Sun-bleached, sweat-stained, the open road.
**Prompt DNA:** `70s New Hollywood, sun-bleached, naturalistic lighting, long lens, American landscape, existential framing, road movie aesthetic, denim and dust`
**References:** Easy Rider, Badlands, Two-Lane Blacktop, Vanishing Point, Terrence Malick.
**Signature moves:** Magic hour everything, lens flare as mood, the car as character, vast American emptiness, golden light on damaged people.

### DIR-18. GASPAR NOE
**Feel:** The camera won't look away. Won't stop spinning. Won't let you leave. Sensory assault as empathy.
**Prompt DNA:** `Gaspar Noe style, extreme long takes, strobing light, neon saturation, disorienting camera rotation, overhead floating shots, confrontational framing, nightclub aesthetic`
**References:** Enter the Void, Irreversible, Climax, Love.
**Signature moves:** Overhead soul-floating camera, the inversion (upside down frame), single-shot endurance, neon as drug, the strobelight sequence, DMT color palette.

### DIR-19. TARSEM SINGH
**Feel:** Every frame a painting. Literally. Art history as production design. Maximum beauty, zero restraint.
**Prompt DNA:** `Tarsem Singh style, painterly composition, art history references in frame, saturated operatic color, costume as architecture, impossible locations, visual maximalism`
**References:** The Fall, The Cell, Mirror Mirror.
**Signature moves:** Dalí in frame, costume that could be sculpture, location as painting, vivid red as anchor, the body as art object, flowing fabric in wind.

### DIR-20. CHRISTOPHER NOLAN
**Feel:** IMAX precision. Practical spectacle. Time as structure. The clockwork reveals itself.
**Prompt DNA:** `Christopher Nolan style, IMAX scale, practical effects, cold blue-grey palette, architectural precision, cross-cut tension, time manipulation visual, suits and concrete`
**References:** Inception, Interstellar, Dunkirk, Tenet, The Dark Knight, Oppenheimer.
**Signature moves:** Rotating sets, IMAX landscape scale, suits in impossible environments, the ticking clock made visual, parallel timelines in frame.

## MOVEMENTS & GENRES

Visual languages defined by cultural movements, not individual directors.

### MOV-1. NEO-NOIR
**Feel:** Shadows have weight. Light is rationed. Someone is lying.
**Prompt DNA:** `neo-noir, hard shadows, neon reflections, wet surfaces, chiaroscuro, high contrast, noir lighting, rain-slicked streets, venetian blind shadows`
**References:** Blade Runner, Drive, Se7en, Chinatown, Nightcrawler, Sin City.

### MOV-2. GOTHIC
**Feel:** Stone, candlelight, old gods. Beauty that bites. The medieval through a nightmare lens.
**Prompt DNA:** `gothic aesthetic, cathedral architecture, candlelight, grotesque detail, gargoyles, ornate darkness, stained glass, dramatic chiaroscuro`
**References:** Berserk, Dark Souls, Guillermo del Toro, Castlevania, Crimson Peak, Bloodborne.

### MOV-3. CYBERPUNK
**Feel:** Rain on chrome. Kanji in puddles. The corporate future that already arrived.
**Prompt DNA:** `cyberpunk, neon-lit rain, holographic ads, dense urban layering, Asian-fusion architecture, smoke and fog, pink and cyan lighting, megastructures`
**References:** Blade Runner, Ghost in the Shell, Akira, Cyberpunk 2077, Mr. Robot, Neuromancer.

### MOV-4. SYNTHWAVE / RETRO-FUTURISM
**Feel:** The future the 1980s promised. Chrome, neon, sunset grids, synthesizer light.
**Prompt DNA:** `synthwave, neon grid, chrome reflections, purple and cyan palette, 80s retro-futurism, sunset gradient, scanlines, VHS tracking`
**References:** Tron: Legacy, Drive (credits), Kavinsky, Hotline Miami.

### MOV-5. SURREALISM
**Feel:** The dream is the logic. Impossible images rendered with conviction.
**Prompt DNA:** `surrealist, impossible physics, dreamlike composition, melting forms, unexpected juxtaposition, hyper-detailed impossible scene, Magritte, Dali`
**References:** Jodorowsky, Dali, Magritte, EEAAO, Annihilation, Un Chien Andalou.

### MOV-6. BRUTALISM
**Feel:** Concrete. Bold. Architecture IS ideology. Nothing decorative. Everything structural.
**Prompt DNA:** `brutalist, raw concrete, geometric minimalism, monolithic structures, harsh overhead lighting, oppressive architecture, institutional scale`
**References:** Severance, High-Rise, Barbican, Le Corbusier, Soviet architecture.

### MOV-7. ART DECO
**Feel:** Geometric luxury. Gold on black. The future as imagined in 1927.
**Prompt DNA:** `art deco, geometric patterns, gold and black palette, luxurious symmetry, 1920s futurism, ornamental precision, chrome and glass, Chrysler Building`
**References:** Metropolis, Bioshock, The Great Gatsby (2013), Tamara de Lempicka.

### MOV-8. MAXIMALISM / BAROQUE
**Feel:** More is more. Every surface filled. Horror vacui. The eye has nowhere to rest.
**Prompt DNA:** `maximalist, ornate details, rich textures, layered decoration, jewel-tone palette, baroque excess, dense visual information, gilded surfaces`
**References:** Moulin Rouge, Grand Budapest Hotel, Jeunet (Amelie), Rococo.

### MOV-9. MINIMALISM
**Feel:** What's not there is the point. One subject. The void speaks.
**Prompt DNA:** `minimalist, vast negative space, single subject isolation, monochromatic, clinical precision, empty environment, clean geometric`
**References:** 2001 (white room), Ex Machina, Under the Skin, Apple, Ryoji Ikeda.

### MOV-10. SOLARPUNK
**Feel:** Green tech. Organic architecture. The optimistic future where nature won.
**Prompt DNA:** `solarpunk, organic architecture, green technology, lush vegetation integrated with structures, warm sunlight, sustainable utopia, living buildings`
**References:** Ghibli (tech + nature), Wakanda gardens, biomimicry design.

### MOV-11. FASHION / EDITORIAL
**Feel:** Beauty as aggression. Composed to sell a feeling, not tell a story.
**Prompt DNA:** `editorial photography, dramatic studio lighting, high fashion, beauty close-up, bold color, glossy finish, magazine quality`
**References:** Nick Knight, Steven Meisel, Helmut Newton, Vogue, Hype Williams.

### MOV-12. LO-FI / ZINE / PUNK
**Feel:** Photocopied. Stapled. Production value IS authenticity.
**Prompt DNA:** `zine aesthetic, photocopied texture, hand-drawn lettering, xerox grain, punk collage, black and white with spot color, lo-fi`
**References:** Riot Grrrl, Pettibon, Basquiat, DIY punk, Supreme.

### MOV-13. WESTERN
**Feel:** Dust, light, the empty frame. Landscape as moral battleground. The horizon is always watching.
**Prompt DNA:** `western, vast desert landscape, dust and golden light, worn leather and wood, frontier architecture, sunset silhouettes, arid earth tones`
**References:** Leone (The Good the Bad the Ugly), Unforgiven, No Country for Old Men, Meek's Cutoff, The Power of the Dog.

### MOV-14. SOUTHERN GOTHIC
**Feel:** Humidity you can see. Decay as beauty. The rot underneath the manners.
**Prompt DNA:** `southern gothic, humid atmosphere, Spanish moss, decaying plantation, dappled green light, swamp fog, weathered wood, oppressive heat haze`
**References:** True Detective S1, Beasts of the Southern Wild, Eve's Bayou, Midnight in the Garden of Good and Evil.

### MOV-15. COSMIC HORROR
**Feel:** Scale that breaks the brain. The thing you see that you shouldn't be able to see. Geometry that's wrong.
**Prompt DNA:** `cosmic horror, impossible scale, non-Euclidean geometry, deep void, bioluminescence in darkness, tentacular forms, overwhelming vastness, Lovecraftian`
**References:** Annihilation, Color Out of Space, Nope (the reveal), Bloodborne, Uzumaki.

## CULTURAL AESTHETICS

Visual languages rooted in specific cultural traditions.

### CUL-1. AFROFUTURISM
**Feel:** African diaspora meets speculative futures. Ancestral knowledge as advanced technology.
**Prompt DNA:** `Afrofuturism, African patterns and textiles, advanced tech with organic forms, gold and earth tones, tribal geometry meets sci-fi, ancestral futurism`
**References:** Black Panther, Janelle Monae, Sun Ra, Octavia Butler, Wangechi Mutu.

### CUL-2. INDIGENOUS FUTURISM
**Feel:** First Nations, Aboriginal, and indigenous visual systems projected forward. Ancient pattern as future code.
**Prompt DNA:** `indigenous futurism, traditional patterns as technology, earth pigment palette, ceremonial geometry, natural materials meets advanced tech`
**References:** Reservation Dogs, Prey (2022), Blood Quantum, Aboriginal dot painting.

### CUL-3. UKIYO-E / JAPANESE PRINT
**Feel:** Flat planes, bold outlines, nature as protagonist. Centuries of visual grammar.
**Prompt DNA:** `ukiyo-e style, Japanese woodblock print, flat color planes, bold black outlines, nature subjects, wave patterns`
**References:** Hokusai, Hiroshige, Okami.

### CUL-4. WUXIA / CHINESE MARTIAL ARTS
**Feel:** Silk in motion. Bamboo forests. Gravity as suggestion. The body as calligraphy.
**Prompt DNA:** `wuxia aesthetic, flowing silk robes, bamboo forest, wire-fu choreography, ink wash atmosphere, misty mountains, martial arts elegance`
**References:** Crouching Tiger Hidden Dragon, Hero (Zhang Yimou), House of Flying Daggers, The Grandmaster.

### CUL-5. BOLLYWOOD / INDIAN MAXIMALISM
**Feel:** Color detonation. Emotion at maximum volume. Gold, silk, monsoon. Spectacle as sincerity.
**Prompt DNA:** `Indian cinematic maximalism, vivid saturated color, gold and jewel tones, ornate costume, monsoon rain, massive crowd choreography, palace interiors`
**References:** Devdas, Baahubali, RRR, Lagaan.

### CUL-6. LATIN MAGIC REALISM
**Feel:** The miraculous inside the ordinary. Flowers growing through concrete. Death sits at the table.
**Prompt DNA:** `Latin magic realism, vibrant warm palette, Dia de los Muertos iconography, flowers and decay, candlelight, folk art texture, the miraculous in the mundane`
**References:** Coco, Pan's Labyrinth, Amores Perros, City of God, Garcia Marquez visual adaptations.

### CUL-7. SCANDINAVIAN / NORDIC
**Feel:** Clean lines, cold light, vast emptiness. Design as philosophy. The silence between the words.
**Prompt DNA:** `Scandinavian aesthetic, cold natural light, minimal design, birch and pine, grey-blue palette, vast empty landscape, functional beauty, muted tones`
**References:** Midsommar, Borgen, The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo, Noma, Swedish design.

### CUL-8. SOVIET / EASTERN BLOC
**Feel:** Concrete utopia gone wrong. The poster is a lie but the architecture is real. Red as ideology.
**Prompt DNA:** `Soviet aesthetic, constructivist design, red and grey palette, monumental architecture, propaganda poster style, industrial decay, brutalist housing blocks`
**References:** Chernobyl (HBO), The Death of Stalin, Stalker (Tarkovsky), constructivist graphic design.

### CUL-9. MIDDLE EASTERN / ARABIAN
**Feel:** Geometric infinity. Calligraphy as architecture. Desert light. Gold and lapis.
**Prompt DNA:** `Arabian aesthetic, Islamic geometric patterns, calligraphy, desert golden light, ornate tilework, courtyard architecture, gold and lapis lazuli, sand dune landscape`
**References:** Arabian Nights, Theeb, Capernaum, Alhambra, Persian miniature painting.

### CUL-10. K-AESTHETIC / KOREAN WAVE
**Feel:** Hyper-modern, skin-forward, the grid of the city as visual language. Clean meets neon. Beauty as system.
**Prompt DNA:** `Korean aesthetic, hyper-clean modern interior, neon-lit streets, K-beauty skin texture, glass and concrete, minimalist luxury, night market glow, pastel and neon`
**References:** Parasite, Squid Game, Oldboy, Korean skincare visual language, Seoul architecture.
