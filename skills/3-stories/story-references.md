# Story References — Principle Buckets + Anti-Patterns

Pattern-matching fuel. Not deep-dive benchmarks. Films organized by what they nail — the structural principle, the genre engine, or the emotional register. Use during generation (3a) for calibration and during development (3b) for quality checking.

---

## By Sauce Mother

### The Premise (Egri Machine: Trait → Action → Consequence)

Films where the one-sentence premise IS the entire story. You can trace every scene back to the causal chain.

| Film | The Premise |
|------|------------|
| *There Will Be Blood* | Ambition unchecked by love → consuming everything → empire and total emptiness |
| *Parasite* | Desperate ingenuity in an unjust system → infiltrating the privileged → the system kills from both ends |
| *No Country for Old Men* | Believing you can outrun consequence → escalating bets → consequence doesn't negotiate |
| *Uncut Gems* | Compulsive gambling with everything → can't stop when ahead → the last bet is your life |
| *Breaking Bad* | Wounded pride masked as family duty → building an empire → pride was the point all along |
| *Nightcrawler* | Sociopathic ambition + media ethics vacuum → manufacturing news → the system rewards the monster |
| *Whiplash* | Obsessive pursuit of greatness → accepting abuse as the price → becoming the abuser |

### The Wound (Need vs. Want Gap)

Films where the internal contradiction IS the engine. Every external event exists to widen or close the gap.

| Film | The Wound |
|------|----------|
| *Moonlight* | Needs to accept his tenderness / wants to be hard enough to survive |
| *Lady Bird* | Needs to see her mother clearly / wants to escape her mother's world |
| *Good Will Hunting* | Needs to let someone in / wants to prove he doesn't need anyone |
| *Eternal Sunshine* | Needs to accept that love includes pain / wants to erase the pain |
| *Manchester by the Sea* | Needs to forgive himself / wants to be left alone with his grief |
| *Aftersun* | Needs to understand her father / wants the memory to stay uncomplicated |
| *Frances Ha* | Needs to grow up / wants the friendship to stay the way it was |

### The Pressure Cooker (Escalating Irreversibility)

Films where the world makes inaction impossible and every choice narrows future options.

| Film | The Pressure Mechanism |
|------|----------------------|
| *Get Out* | Social pressure IS the trap — every "reasonable" choice takes Chris deeper |
| *Uncut Gems* | Each bet funds the next — the spiral tightens with every scene |
| *Gravity* | Oxygen depleting, debris field returning — physics as progressive complication |
| *Mad Max: Fury Road* | The pursuit never stops — forward is the only option |
| *Whiplash* | Fletcher's escalating demands — each victory raises the bar, never satisfies |
| *Sicario* | Each crossing takes Kate deeper into a system she can't exit |
| *A Quiet Place* | Every sound is a death sentence — the world itself is the pressure cooker |

### The Collision (Hinge Moment: Discovery + Reversal)

Films where one moment splits the story into before and after. The protagonist can't un-know it.

| Film | The Collision |
|------|-------------|
| *The Truman Show* | Truman reaches the wall. The sky is painted. Life was manufactured. |
| *Incendies* | The twins discover who their father is. The math doesn't work until it horrifyingly does. |
| *Get Out* | Chris sees the photos. Every "kind" gesture was assessment. The auction was always running. |
| *Arrival* | Louise realizes the visions are memories of the future. Language rewired time. |
| *The Sixth Sense* | Malcolm realizes he's been dead the entire time. Every scene rewrites. |
| *Fight Club* | The narrator IS Tyler Durden. The destruction was self-inflicted. |
| *Black Swan* | Nina can't distinguish performance from psychosis. The perfection IS the breakdown. |
| *Gone Girl* | Amy is alive. The victim was the architect. The marriage was a crime scene. |

### The Cost (What's Gained, Lost, and Understood)

Films where the resolution costs something real and the audience leaves with emotional clarification, not just emotional release.

| Film | Gained | Lost | Understood |
|------|--------|------|-----------|

| *12 Years a Slave* | Freedom | 12 years, and every person he couldn't save | Freedom has a survivor's weight |
| *Eternal Sunshine* | Clementine, again | Certainty that it'll work | Love is choosing someone knowing exactly how it breaks |
| *The Florida Project* | Moonee's childhood, for a while | The childhood itself — the fantasy cannot hold | Joy and precarity coexist. Neither cancels the other. |
| *Hereditary* | Nothing | Every family member | Grief as possession — the thing that takes you over was always inside the family |

## By Genre Engine

Structural machines from Snyder and Truby. Each engine has specific required elements — these aren't just vibes.

### Monster in the House
**Required:** A monster (created by sin), a house (confined space), a sin (that brought the monster).

| Film | Monster | House | Sin |
|------|---------|-------|-----|
| *Alien* | The xenomorph | The Nostromo | Corporate greed (Weyland-Yutani rerouted the ship) |
| *Get Out* | The Armitage family | The estate / the sunken place | Liberal racism — the compliment IS the consumption |
| *Jaws* | The shark | Amity Island (kept open for tourism) | Greed — the mayor refused to close the beach |
| *A Quiet Place* | The creatures | The soundless world | Unclear origin — the sin is pre-story, the world inherits it |
| *The Thing* | The alien that imitates | The Antarctic research station | Isolation + distrust — the monster feeds on paranoia |
| *Hereditary* | Paimon / the family curse | The family home | Generational — the grandmother's deal |

### Institutionalized
**Required:** A group, a choice (join or leave), a sacrifice or branding.

| Film | The Institution | The Choice | The Cost |
|------|----------------|-----------|---------|
| *Shawshank* | Prison | Become institutionalized or maintain hope | Brooks couldn't survive outside |
| *Whiplash* | Shaffer Conservatory / Fletcher | Accept abuse as the price of greatness | Andrew becomes Fletcher |
| *Full Metal Jacket* | The Marines | Become the machine or break | Pyle breaks. Joker bends. |
| *The Godfather* | The Corleone family | Michael chooses the family business | Michael becomes what he swore he wouldn't |
| *One Flew Over the Cuckoo's Nest* | The mental institution | McMurphy fights it | The institution lobotomizes him |
| *The Social Network* | Facebook / Harvard | Zuckerberg chooses the product over every relationship | Alone in a room, refreshing the page |

### Whydunit
**Required:** A detective (can be amateur), a secret, a dark turn (the why is worse than the what).

| Film | The Why |
|------|---------|
| *Gone Girl* | Amy did it because the marriage was a performance and she decided to win |
| *Zodiac* | We never learn why — the absence of the answer IS the horror |
| *Nightcrawler* | Lou does it because the system rewards sociopathy — the why is the system itself |
| *Killers of the Flower Moon* | Hale did it for oil money — the why is banal, which makes it monstrous |

### Out of the Bottle
**Required:** A wish, a spell (the mechanism), a lesson (usually: be careful what you wish for).

| Film | The Wish | What Goes Wrong |
|------|----------|----------------|
| *Groundhog Day* | Phil gets to relive the day | Must become worthy of the day to escape it |
| *Being John Malkovich* | Access to another person's consciousness | Everyone wants in — identity dissolves |
| *Everything Everywhere All at Once* | Evelyn accesses infinite selves | Every self is a reminder of the life she didn't choose |
| *Eternal Sunshine* | Joel erases Clementine | He realizes mid-erasure he doesn't want to forget |

### Horror (as Engine)
**Required:** Violation of natural order → escalating dread → confrontation with the impossible.

| Film | The Violation |
|------|-------------|
| *The Shining* | The Overlook absorbs Jack — isolation becomes possession |
| *Hereditary* | The grief opens a door that was always there — the family was chosen before they were born |
| *Midsommar* | A community that performs care while consuming outsiders |
| *Under the Skin* | An alien learns human sensation — the horror is empathy awakening in a predator |
| *It Follows* | Sex transmits an inescapable follower — intimacy as doom |
| *Perfect Blue* | Identity fractures — Mima can't tell which version of herself is real |

### Fool Triumphant
**Required:** A fool (underestimated), an establishment (threatened by the fool), a transmutation (the fool's "weakness" becomes their power).

| Film | The Fool | The Establishment |
|------|----------|------------------|
| *Forrest Gump* | Forrest — simple, literal, earnest | Every institution he stumbles through (army, business, politics) |
| *Being There* | Chance — gardener mistaken for sage | Washington political elite |
| *The Big Lebowski* | The Dude — aggressively unbothered | Everyone with an agenda (nihilists, pornographers, millionaires) |
| *Elf* | Buddy — raised by elves, believes literally | Cynical New York |

## By Emotional Register

### Obsession
The tightening spiral. Audience watches someone accelerate toward their own destruction.

*There Will Be Blood*, *Whiplash*, *Phantom Thread*, *Black Swan*, *Amadeus*, *Requiem for a Dream*, *Zodiac*, *Nightcrawler*, *Pi*, *Moby Dick*

### Fall from Grace
Rise-then-collapse. The audience knows it's coming.

*Goodfellas*, *The Social Network*, *Scarface*, *Sunset Boulevard*, *Breaking Bad*, *Boogie Nights*, *Casino*, *Barry Lyndon*

### Escape
Mounting claustrophobia → the break → the cost.

*Shawshank*, *Mad Max: Fury Road*, *Snowpiercer*, *Room*, *The Truman Show*, *12 Years a Slave*, *A Quiet Place*, *The Handmaid's Tale*

### Forbidden Love
The obstacle IS that the love is transgressive.

*Brokeback Mountain*, *The Handmaiden*, *Carol*, *Call Me by Your Name*, *In the Mood for Love*, *Romeo + Juliet*, *Portrait of a Lady on Fire*, *Moonlight*

### Outsider
Doesn't belong. Can't leave. Won't conform.

*Edward Scissorhands*, *Frances Ha*, *The Florida Project*, *Moonlight*, *District 9*, *Parasite*, *The Worst Person in the World*, *Napoleon Dynamite*

### Madness
The protagonist's grip on reality loosens — or was never there.

*Black Swan*, *Requiem for a Dream*, *Pi*, *Perfect Blue*, *The Shining*, *Eraserhead*, *Mulholland Drive*, *Joker*, *Taxi Driver*

### Quest
The search gives direction. Every obstacle lands because the audience knows what winning looks like.

*Dune*, *Lord of the Rings*, *Finding Nemo*, *WALL-E*, *Mad Max: Fury Road*, *Star Wars*, *True Grit*, *Moana*, *2001*

### Revenge
Righteous fury that slowly corrupts.

*Oldboy*, *Kill Bill*, *Blue Ruin*, *Promising Young Woman*, *Django Unchained*, *The Count of Monte Cristo*, *Gladiator*, *Sicario*

## Anti-Patterns (Expanded)

### Seed-Level

| Pattern | What It Looks Like | The Fix |
|---------|-------------------|---------|
| **Thesis Paper** | "It's about the ethics of AI" | State as Egri machine: "[Trait] leads to [action] which results in [consequence]" |
| **Tour Guide** | Protagonist experiences the world | Give them a wound. Make inaction impossible. Force a collision. |
| **Explainer** | Story delivers worldbuilding | Mamet: what does the protagonist WANT? As a verb. |
| **One-Trick** | World mechanic IS the story | Find the human story underneath. Independence test. |
| **Chosen One** | Special because plot says so | Specialness must come FROM the flaw — the wound creates the power |

### Arc-Level

| Pattern | What It Looks Like | The Fix |
|---------|-------------------|---------|
| **Symmetrical Opponent** | Antagonist is just protagonist's mirror | Give opponent independent valid claim |
| **Inert Collision** | Discovery happens TO protagonist | Discovery must come FROM protagonist's actions |
| **Clean Resolution** | Gains everything, loses nothing | Name the specific cost. Make it permanent. |
| **The Drift** | Generic story wearing the world as costume | Reground: name factions, systems, rules that shape events |
| **The Lecture** | Resolution delivers a moral | Show consequence. Don't state meaning. Audience does that. |
| **Wound Amnesia** | Arc forgets the need/want gap halfway through | The wound should intensify, not disappear. Every beat should touch it. |
| **Compression Failure** | Three acts feel like three different stories | The premise connects them. If it doesn't, the premise is wrong. |
