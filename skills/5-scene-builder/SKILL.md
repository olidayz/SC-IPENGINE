# 5-SCENE-BUILDER

Generate a world's scene library. 250+ ideas. Every one iconic.

This skill generates WHAT EXISTS in the world. Not how to film it. Cinematography, camera language, sound design, and shot composition belong to 6-shots downstream. The scene builder's job is to identify the strongest images, moments, artifacts, and fragments a world contains — then present them for curation.

---

## Anti-Patterns (Read First)

| Anti-Pattern | Why It Kills |
|---|---|
| **Generic fantasy/sci-fi imagery** | If the scene could exist in any IP, it doesn't belong in THIS IP. The specificity of the world is the whole point. |
| **Exposition without feeling** | If the scene's primary function is to EXPLAIN something, it's a textbook. Exposition rides on emotion or it dies. |
| **Heroic composition** | No hero silhouettes, no epic low-angles, no cape-in-wind, no triumph lighting. These are VLD anti-prompts for most Spacecadet worlds. Check the world's VLD. |
| **Single-texture scenes** | A scene that is ONLY dark, ONLY funny, ONLY sad, ONLY weird is flat. Internal contradiction makes scenes rewatchable. |
| **Summary instead of image** | "A tense confrontation between two factions" is a summary. "Two people in identical costumes on opposite sides of a plexiglass counter, one with a permit badge, one without" is a scene. |
| **Forcing scenes into spines** | Spines are analyzed AFTER all generation is complete. Never generate a scene BECAUSE it fits a spine. |
| **Over-indexing one territory** | If you've generated 8 scenes in one location, move. If every scene features the protagonist, widen. Variety proves reality. |
| **Cinematography creep** | If you're describing camera angles, aspect ratios, lighting rigs, or sound design — stop. That's 6-shots. This skill generates the IDEA, not the shot. |

## Scene Schema

Every scene in the library uses this format:

```
**[###] TITLE**
*Hook line — one sentence. The image AND the emotional thesis. Specific enough to see, resonant enough to feel.*
```

Two lines. That's it.

**The title** is evocative, not descriptive. It names the scene with enough specificity to distinguish it from every other scene, and enough mystery to pull the reader in. Titles should do double duty — informational AND emotional.

**The hook line** carries the whole scene in one sentence. It must contain: (1) a specific visual image — something you can SEE, and (2) the emotional payload — why this image matters. The best hook lines create internal contradiction within the sentence itself.

**What does NOT belong here:**
- Camera angles, aspect ratios, composition cues → 6-shots
- Sound design, audio direction, sonic texture → 6-shots
- Lighting specifications, color grades → 6-shots
- Spine tags → Spine Report (after all invocations)

### Scene Quality Standard

A hook line passes if you can read title + hook in 3 seconds and immediately know: what you're looking at, why it's interesting, and whether you want to make it.

A hook line fails if: it's a plot summary, it requires world-bible knowledge to understand, it describes a MOOD rather than an IMAGE, or it could belong to any IP.

### World Media Scenes

World media scenes are fragments of content produced BY entities INSIDE the civilization — intercepted as if leaked into our timeline. A news broadcast, a corporate training video, a GRWM, a Reddit post, a PSA. The audience recognizes the FORMAT instantly — that recognition is free engagement. The IP content inside the format is the payload.

World media scenes use Cultural Instruments — 128+ recognizable media formats catalogued in `references/cultural-instruments.md`. Instruments can be applied three ways:

1. **Entity-driven:** A media-producing institution creates content using a format natural to it.
2. **Character-driven:** A character uses a format — a protagonist filming a confessional, a faction leader doing a GRWM, a janitor recording a voice memo.
3. **Format-collision:** Two instruments fused, or a format applied to content it was never designed for.

All five invocations can generate world media scenes.

## Scene Generation Principles

How to make every scene incredible.

**1. SURFACES TELL STORIES.**
The most powerful world-building is in texture, not action. Injection-molded polymer tells a different story than hand-stitched leather. A Pantone color swatch tells a different story than spray paint. Materials are characters.

**2. THE MUNDANE IS MORE INTERESTING THAN THE SPECTACULAR.**
A permit application is more interesting than a fight. A gift shop at the end of a wound is more interesting than the wound. A wash protocol sign is more interesting than the costume. Find the institutional, bureaucratic, absurd details inside extraordinary premises.

**3. CONTRAST IS KING.**
Internal contradiction within a single image. Beauty AND horror, comedy AND grief, mundane AND sacred in one hook line. The best scenes contain two things that shouldn't coexist.

**4. THE THRESHOLD IS THE SCENE.**
The moment of crossing between zones is often more interesting than what's on either side. Privilege transition moments.

**5. ABSENCE IS PRESENCE.**
What's MISSING is often the most powerful detail. An empty hook where a costume should be. A signal that calls nobody. A hand reaching toward a drawer and stopping. The void is the subject.

**6. PERSPECTIVE IS EVERYTHING.**
Who is LOOKING at this scene? The insider, the outsider, the bystander, the infrastructure worker, the child, the camera that nobody knows about? The perspective choice IS the scene's point of view — even if camera angles belong downstream, the WHOSE EYES question belongs here.

## The 7 Gates

Every scene must pass before entering the library.

| Gate | Test | Kill If... |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Iconic Frame** | Does the hook line contain an image that could be a poster? | You can't identify one screenshot-worthy image. |
| **2. World-Building Payload** | Does this scene teach you something about the world? | Beautiful but empty. A mood reel frame, not a world frame. |
| **3. Emotional Specificity** | Can you name the emotion from the hook line alone? | The feeling is vague — "dramatic," "intense," "emotional." |
| **4. Contradiction** | Does the scene contain internal friction between two registers? | Single-texture — only scary, only funny, only beautiful. |
| **5. Sensory Texture** | Does the hook line invoke a textured environment — not just visual but material? | The scene feels flat — no temperature, no surface, no air. |
| **6. Promise Break** | Does it subvert an expectation? | Mandatory in Invocations 3+5. Encouraged in all others. |
| **7. Independence** | Would this hook someone with zero world context? | Requires lore to care about. |

## Spine Candidates

Spines are recurring threads that hold a piece of content together — visual motifs, thematic questions, character echoes, object recurrences, material textures.

**Generate 4-6 spine candidates BEFORE scene generation in Invocation 1.** Derive them from the world's strongest visual/thematic elements. They might be:

- A recurring visual motif (a color, a symbol, an object appearing in different contexts)
- Objects that exist in multiple versions (the same artifact in different hands)
- A recurring character presence (one character glimpsed across many settings)
- A recurring question (the same question asked by different voices)
- A tonal fault line (the specific shift between two registers that defines this world)
- A material/texture thread (one substance appearing in every faction's territory)

**Never generate scenes TO FIT a spine.** Spines are for the Spine Report at the end — not for scene generation. Generate the best scenes possible. The spines reveal themselves.

## The Five Invocations

Each invocation reads the world package, generates ~50 scenes, applies the 7 Gates. Each invocation produces a MIX of cinematic scenes AND world media artifacts — no invocation is type-locked.

### Invocation 1: EXTRACT + EXPAND

Working from what exists in the material.

→ Load `5a-extract-expand.md`
→ Load `references/cultural-instruments.md` (for world media scenes)

Extract every scene that's explicit or implied in the world package. Expand into new scenes the material points toward but doesn't describe. Also generate world media scenes using Cultural Instruments — leaning toward entities and characters the material already names, but not restricted to them.

Output: ~50 scenes + Exclusion List.

### Invocation 2: GENERATE + INVENT

Working from what SHOULD exist but doesn't yet.

→ Load `5b-generate-invent.md`
→ Load `references/cultural-instruments.md` (for instrument selection)

Two parallel modes: (1) New scenes the material doesn't imply but the world demands. (2) Invent new media entities, generate world media scenes using all three application modes (entity-driven, character-driven, format-collision). Target 25-35 Cultural Instruments total.

Input includes Exclusion List from Invocation 1.
Output: ~50 scenes + combined Exclusion List.

### Invocation 3: WEIRD TERRITORY

Working from what nobody expects.

→ Load `5c-weird-territory.md`
→ Load `references/cultural-instruments.md` (for format collisions)

All obvious territory from Invocations 1+2 is excluded. Three forcing functions: Collision (fuse instruments, locations, characters, formats), Genre Inversion (flip the established tone), The Unseen (3:47 AM, the boring hours, taboo subjects, non-human perspectives, children's dreams). Gate 6 (Promise Break) is MANDATORY for every scene.

Input includes combined Exclusion List from Invocations 1+2.
Output: ~50 scenes + combined Exclusion List.

### Invocation 4: PARTICIPATION

Working from how audiences JOIN this world.

→ Load `5d-participation.md`
→ Load `references/cultural-instruments.md`

Scenes designed for fan entry: wearable objects, faction declarations, rituals, signals, user-generated content, open-registry actions, physical artifacts. Every scene should make the viewer think "I want to DO that" or "I want to OWN that" or "I want to BE in that faction." The world's "How Fans Join" section is the primary source, but invention is encouraged.

Input includes combined Exclusion List from Invocations 1-3.
Output: ~50 scenes + combined Exclusion List.

### Invocation 5: COUNTER-NARRATIVE

Working from friction, dissent, and the world's own shadow.

→ Load `5e-counter-narrative.md`
→ Load `references/cultural-instruments.md`

Scenes from people who hate the premise, mourn the old version, see through the system, or refuse the world's logic. The skeptic, the nostalgist, the person who was displaced, the person who profits and knows it's wrong. Every world has people who push back — their perspective is the test of the world's integrity. Gate 6 (Promise Break) is MANDATORY for every scene.

Input includes combined Exclusion List from Invocations 1-4.
Output: ~50 scenes.

## Exclusion List Format

After each invocation (except the last), output a compact territory summary. This feeds into the next invocation to prevent duplication.

```
EXCLUSION LIST — INVOCATION [N]
TERRITORY COVERED:
- [Location/character/concept]: [what angles were explored] (x[N] scenes)
...
[Full list of scene titles for reference]
```

The next invocation reads this and generates scenes that explore DIFFERENT angles, locations, characters, and textures.

## Spine Report

After ALL five invocations are complete, output a Spine Report. This replaces per-scene spine tagging.

```
═══════════════════════════════════════════
SPINE REPORT
═══════════════════════════════════════════

SPINE CANDIDATES (from Invocation 1):
1. [Spine name] — [1-sentence description]
2. [Spine name] — [1-sentence description]
...

SPINE DENSITY:
[Spine name]: ~[N] scenes connect naturally
[Spine name]: ~[N] scenes connect naturally
...

RECOMMENDED SPINES (top 2-3):
1. [Spine name] — [why it's the strongest: density + quality + emotional range]
2. [Spine name] — [why]
3. [Spine name] — [why]

EMERGENT SPINES (discovered during generation):
- [New spine] — [description, which invocation surfaced it, approximate density]
...
```

The Spine Report is strategic — it tells the editor which threads to pull. It's not scene-level metadata; it's a recommendation layer.

## Internal Verification (After Each Invocation)

Silently check the scene library against anti-patterns.

**Check for:**
- Territory clustering (too many scenes in one location/character?)
- Texture variety (cinematic AND world media in this invocation?)
- Gate compliance (does every scene pass all 7?)
- Hook quality (does every hook line contain a specific image AND an emotional payload?)
- Cinematography creep (are you describing shots instead of ideas?)

**If all pass:** "50 scenes generated. [One sentence on strongest quality]. Ready for Invocation [N+1] or user curation."

**If any concern:** Surface it and suggest adjustment.
