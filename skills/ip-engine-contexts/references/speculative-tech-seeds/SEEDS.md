# Speculative Tech Seeds

Seeds are raw material for transformation. Not a reference database — an evolution engine.

## Gates

Every seed must pass all four before becoming a Central Premise. Fail any = reject.

| Gate | Test |
|------|------|
| **Load-Bearing** | Does this tech define how the world works, not just decorate it? |
| **Unrecognizable** | Would the original author fail to recognize their concept? |
| **Conflict-Generating** | Does this tech create structural tension (scarcity, inequality, paradox)? |
| **Multi-System** | Does this tech ripple into economy, governance, identity, or culture? |

**Anti-Patterns:** Tech as easter egg or gadget. Naming or citing source seeds. One tech per world (layer 2-3 as infrastructure). Loading more than 2 seed files per session.

## Transformation Flow

```
SEED (from library)
    ↓  "What if this wasn't a gadget — what if it was the entire world?"
EVOLUTION (mutate, combine, invert, scale)
    ↓  Apply four gates
CENTRAL PREMISE (the axis the world turns on)
```

### Evolution Moves

| Move | Method | Example |
|------|--------|---------|
| **Scale** | Gadget → infrastructure | Neural Lace → all thought is networked |
| **Invert** | Flip the premise | Teleportation → you arrive wrong every time |
| **Combine** | Layer 2-3 seeds | Flesh Putty + Nerve Machine → body is currency, pain is law |
| **Weaponize** | Who controls it? | Memory editing → who owns your past? |
| **Democratize** | Everyone has it | Precognition → no one can be surprised |

## Examples

### Single Seed → Central Premise (PASS)

**Seed:** Chairdog (seeds_body_life / Enhancement) — A living, sentient chair.

**Evolution:** What if ALL objects were semi-sentient? Your possessions remember everything. Furniture refuses service.

**Central Premise:** All objects are witnesses. Your bed knows your dreams. Your walls hear your arguments. Privacy is extinct — not from surveillance, but because your possessions have opinions.

| Gate | Result |
|------|--------|
| Load-Bearing | ✅ Defines daily life, architecture, law |
| Unrecognizable | ✅ "Living chair" → "objects as witnesses" |
| Conflict-Generating | ✅ Privacy vs. comfort; testimony vs. loyalty |
| Multi-System | ✅ Economy (objects valued by knowledge), Justice (objects testify), Identity (you are what your things say) |

### Multi-Seed Layering (PASS)

| Layer | Seed | Source | Evolution |
|-------|------|--------|-----------|
| Economy | Flesh Putty | body_life / Enhancement | Body mass is currency — you spend yourself |
| Justice | Nerve Machine | body_life / Enhancement | Pain settles disputes — suffering is law |
| Religion | Gansas | transport / Personal Flight | Death is migration — consciousness carried to orbit |

**World:** You spend your flesh, settle debts in pain, and die by being carried to the sky. The rich are massive, numb, and grounded. The poor are thin, raw-nerved, and waiting for flight.

### Gadget Trap (FAIL → FIX)

**Seed:** Infinite Improbability Drive (space_time / Dimensional)

**❌ Bad:** "This world has a device that makes improbable things happen." (Gadget. Recognizable. No systems.)

**✅ Good:** "Probability is a natural resource. Some regions are probability-rich. Others are probability-deserts. People migrate toward chaos or stability by temperament. Governments regulate the probability gradient." (Passes all four gates.)
