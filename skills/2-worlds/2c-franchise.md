# 2C-FRANCHISE

Stage 3 of world expansion. The world becomes a multi-format, multi-decade IP.

**Key Principle:** Not "what formats could this IP be in" — what formats does this world NATURALLY generate? Every expansion should feel inevitable given the world's infrastructure, not bolted on.

**Presentation Principle:** Don't assume the user remembers all Stage 1/2 terminology. When referencing factions, characters, or world elements, include a brief inline reminder of what they are.

---

## Tonal Range

Can the world sustain different emotional registers? Present as short prose — one sentence per register, with a reference-point comparison. Not a table.

- **Core** — The default tone of the franchise
- **Light** — The comedic/hopeful direction — how far can it go without breaking?
- **Dark** — The serious/intense direction — how far can it go without losing the audience?
- **Weird** — The experimental direction — what's the strangest story this world could tell?

---

## Expansion Vectors

What formats does this world naturally generate? 6 formats: 3 films, a series, a game, and short-form / VV.

| Format | Pitch |
|--------|-------|

- **Film 1:** The entry story — name which faction this enters through, and remind us what they are
- **Film 2-3:** Where the franchise goes next — what part of the world opens up. Each sequel should reveal new infrastructure.
- **Series:** The ongoing story — which faction/conflict sustains episodic tension, and why that conflict doesn't resolve
- **Game:** The native gameplay loop — what system from the world becomes a mechanic
- **Short-form / VV:** The micro-myth — the 60-second version, what world element works at this scale

Each pitch should end with one line that makes the format feel inevitable for THIS world — not a separate column, just the last sentence of the pitch.

**Quality check:** If you can swap out the world name and the pitch still works, the format isn't native enough.

---

## How Fans Join

How do fans enter and participate in this world? Participation + physical objects. One sentence per path. This is a proof-of-concept at this stage, not a marketing plan.

| Path | What It Is |
|------|-----------|

Paths to consider (use what the world naturally generates — not all will apply):
- **Affiliation** — faction/role/identity fans can claim
- **Rituals** — repeatable actions that make fans part of the world
- **Signature Object** — the ONE physical thing that crystallizes the world
- **Creation** — how fans contribute to or extend the world
- **Wearables / Display** — faction signals, functional objects, items that bridge fiction and reality

**Quality check:** "Buy merch" is not participation. Every path should be something only THIS world's fans would do.

**Benchmark:** Star Wars (501st Legion, Galaxy's Edge, lightsaber building), Warhammer 40K (miniature painting, army building), Star Trek (real-world career inspiration, constructed languages).

---

## Expansion Prompts

After presenting all sections, offer 3-4 contextual expansion options based on which areas have the most latent depth. These should be specific to the world just built, not generic.

---

## Reference Check (Invisible)

After completing all sections, silently benchmark against `world-references.md`:

- **Participation:** Are there paths to JOIN, not just consume? (Star Wars, Warhammer, StarCraft benchmarks)
- **Scale:** Could this sustain 3+ entries and decades of expansion? (Star Wars, Dune benchmarks)
- **Play:** Is there a 30-second native gameplay loop? (Warhammer, StarCraft benchmarks)
- **Tonal Flexibility:** Can the franchise do comedy AND tragedy? (Star Wars, Rick & Morty, Spider-Verse benchmarks)
- **Anti-patterns:** Participation barriers? Tonal narrowness? Nostalgia dependency?

If any dimension is weak, flag it in prose. Don't display as a gate table.

---

## Final Verification (Invisible)

Run the complete expansion (all three stages) against `world-references.md`. Do NOT present as a checklist or gate table.

**Internal checks:**
- Gates (Robustness, Belief, Story, Icon, Play, Scale, Title, Reference Calibration)
- Anti-Patterns scan
- Spacecadet dimension check (minimum 2 dimensions naturally present across the full expansion — may emerge from World Premise, Speculative Layer, or world infrastructure. Do not force.)

**If everything passes:** Close with a brief confidence line: "This world is ready for Renders — [one sentence on its strongest quality]."

**If something fails:** Surface the specific concern in natural prose with a suggested fix. Don't list the full gate results.
